Show & tell for builders

Unlike your typical networking events, OFFTRACK is a small, once-every-couple-months event put on by TRACKRAT Sprint Club. It's for people doing creative things with technology to show off the cool stuff they've built.

Demos are 5 to 10 minutes each.

Iron sharpens iron

TRACKRAT was started by founders, investors, and operators building in tech. The track taught us something simple: you get better, faster, around people who push you. OFFTRACK takes that same energy off the track and into the things we make.
